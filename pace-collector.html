How it works. Every 60 seconds it reads the live Supreme Court display board and, whenever a court moves to a new item, records the time and the item's position in that court's declared sequence. Over a week this shows each court's real disposal speed by time of day. Phases: hearing normal list, ment. mentioning (800s), pron. judgement (1500s), idle not sitting — the analysis ignores the non-hearing phases.

When asked, hit Copy all (or Download) and paste it back into the chat. Your data stays in this browser (localStorage) until you clear it.
